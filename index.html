About

Fifteen years of
making it happen

I started in administration and quality control in the Philippines, learning that the detail is the work. Everything since has been a version of that lesson.

From a VVIP casino floor at City of Dreams Manila to a supervisor's desk at Okada. From SMDC, where I closed PHP 150 million in property in 45 days and supervised more than thirty brokers, to Dubai, where I ran acquisitions, leasing and operations for a property portfolio.

Then marketing found me. At AVANTGARDE I worked across destination campaigns for Abu Dhabi, luxury activations for FENDI, Charlotte Tilbury and Diptyque, and brand experiences for GAGGENAU and Lexus. From 2022 to May 2026 I served as Operations Director, holding Sales, Marketing, Admin, Accounts, Operations and HR — through the hardest years of my life. I learned that leadership is mostly the discipline of continuing to show up.
